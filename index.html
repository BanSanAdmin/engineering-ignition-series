About the Author

Dr. Rahul
B. Adhao

Dr. Rahul B. Adhao is an engineering educator, researcher, mentor, and author with 13 years of professional experience, including 12 years in teaching and one year in industry, as stated in his resume.

He currently serves as an Associate Professor at the School of Computation Sciences, JSPM University, Wagholi, Pune. His academic journey includes COEP Technological University and MIT Academy of Engineering, Alandi.

His interests include computer engineering, cybersecurity, research, student mentorship, and career development. He is also a certified NLP Life Coach and has completed the IITM Pravartak Career Counsellor Certification.

Through the Engineering Blueprint series, he aims to help students develop the technical skills, mindset, confidence, and values needed to build successful careers and meaningful lives.
