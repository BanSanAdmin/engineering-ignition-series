About the Author

Dr. Rahul B. Adhao

Engineering Educator, Researcher, Mentor, and Author

Dr. Rahul B. Adhao is an engineering educator, researcher, mentor, and author with 13 years of professional experience, including 12 years in teaching and one year in industry.

He currently serves as an Associate Professor at the School of Computation Sciences, JSPM University, Wagholi, Pune. His academic journey includes COEP Technological University and MIT Academy of Engineering, Alandi.

His interests include computer engineering, cybersecurity, research, student mentorship, and career development. He is a certified NLP Life Coach and has completed the IITM Pravartak Career Counsellor Certification.

Through the Engineering Blueprint series, he aims to help students develop the technical skills, mindset, confidence, and values needed to build successful careers and meaningful lives.

A personal note

A Letter to Every Engineering Aspirant

Dear Engineer-in-the-Making,

First of all, congratulations!

You are about to begin one of the most exciting journeys of your life. Engineering is not just about earning a degree; it is about becoming a problem solver, an innovator, a lifelong learner, and a responsible citizen. If you are reading this book before taking admission, you are already ahead of many others - not because you know more, but because you have chosen to prepare. I don't know your name yet, but I know a few things about you already. You've probably just finished one of the hardest exams of your life - CET, JEE, or both - and somewhere in the last few weeks, someone congratulated you, someone else asked "which branch, which college," and you smiled and answered even though you weren't entirely sure yourself. That's normal. Almost nobody your age is entirely sure. Anyone who tells you they had it all figured out at seventeen is usually remembering it more cleanly than it actually happened.

You already have something students ten years ago didn't. Google, YouTube, AI tools that can explain any concept, solve any equation, debug any code. Use this without guilt. But notice what these tools can't give you: they can't tell you which opportunities are worth your limited time, they can't make you show up when you don't feel like it, they can't build your character. That part is still entirely, unavoidably yours.

Google can answer questions. Artificial Intelligence can explain concepts. But only you can decide what kind of engineer - and what kind of person - you want to become. That decision begins today. I sincerely hope this book becomes your companion throughout your engineering journey.

With best wishes,
Dr. Rahul B. Adhao
Your Mentor
